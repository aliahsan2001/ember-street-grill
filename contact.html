    Good to know

    Questions
    we get a lot

    Where do you deliver?

    Clifton, DHA Phases 1–8, Bath Island and Defence View. Delivery is Rs 150, and free on orders over Rs 3,000. Minimum order for delivery is Rs 800.

    How do I pay?

    Cash on delivery, bank transfer or card when you pick up. Pick one when you send your order.

    How fast is delivery?

    About 25 minutes in Clifton and 35–45 minutes for DHA. It's slower on Friday and Saturday nights, and we'll tell you on WhatsApp if there's a wait.

    Can I book a table?

    Yes. Use the form to send us the date, time and number of guests. Weekends fill up fast, so book a day ahead.

    Do you cater events?

    We do live BBQ stations and platters for 20 to 300 guests. Send the date and headcount and we'll put together a menu.
