Our story

In 2014, Bilal and his uncle Rafiq rolled a charcoal cart onto Boat Basin with one dish: beef seekh kebab, minced by hand that afternoon.

By ten at night there was a queue. By midnight they had sold out.

We still cook the same way. No gas grills and no shortcuts. Every kebab goes over real lump charcoal, every karahi is fired in iron on a high flame, and every marinade sits for at least twelve hours.

What changed is how you reach us. Our phones couldn't keep up with the dinner rush, and our Instagram menu was a stack of photos nobody could search. So we built a menu you can order from directly, without paying a delivery app for the privilege.

Inside the kitchen

How the
flavour gets in

No secret sauce. Just three rules we've never broken.

01

Real lump charcoal

Lit at 3 pm every day and kept at the right heat with a hand fan, not a gas valve.

02

Twelve-hour marinades

Yoghurt, spice and time. Tikka and malai boti are marinated the night before they're grilled.

03

Iron karahi, high flame

Every karahi is cooked to order in seasoned iron, so it reaches you in under 25 minutes.
